# ZAN Website — Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview

ZAN is a Saudi investment company operating across multiple sectors.

The company focuses on owning, managing, developing, and presenting investment projects and opportunities, while building relationships with investors, strategic partners, companies, government and semi-government entities, and financing organizations.

The website is a professional corporate presence for ZAN.

It should communicate trust, professionalism, stability, investment capability, execution, growth, and a strong position in the Saudi market.

The website should NOT position ZAN as a traditional service agency.

Services should be presented as part of ZAN's broader investment and project ecosystem.

---

## 2. Project Goal

The website should:

- Establish a strong and credible digital presence for ZAN.
- Clearly communicate what ZAN is and what it does.
- Present projects, investments, and investment opportunities.
- Present strategic partners.
- Explain ZAN's capabilities and services within its investment ecosystem.
- Encourage investors and strategic partners to contact ZAN.
- Provide an official communication channel for business inquiries.
- Be suitable for investors, companies, government entities, and strategic partners.

---

## 3. Target Audience

Primary audiences:

- Investors
- Strategic partners
- Major companies
- Government and semi-government entities
- Financing organizations
- Project owners
- Investment opportunity owners
- Potential business clients and partners

---

## 4. Phase 1 Scope

Phase 1 is a responsive corporate frontend website.

Pages:

1. Home
2. About
3. Investments & Projects
4. Investment Opportunities
5. Services
6. Strategic Partners
7. Investors & Partnerships
8. Contact

The website will support three languages:

- Arabic
- English
- Russian

Arabic is RTL.
English and Russian are LTR.

---

## 5. Phase 1 Technical Scope

Phase 1 should remain primarily frontend-focused.

Expected:

- Responsive website
- React
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- Client-side routing
- Three-language interface
- Reusable components
- Static/local data structures
- Basic contact form integration
- Basic SEO
- Performance-conscious implementation
- HTTPS through deployment platform
- Custom domain: zanglobal.sa

---

## 6. Explicitly Out of Scope for Phase 1

Do NOT implement:

- Database
- Authentication system
- Admin dashboard
- CMS
- User management
- Advanced roles and permissions
- Persistent request management
- Advanced notifications
- Analytics dashboard
- Advanced reporting
- Advanced search/filtering systems
- Complex backend functionality

These may be considered in future phases.

---

## 7. Content Strategy

Final company content is not ready yet.

During development, temporary placeholder content will be used.

Placeholder content should:

- Have realistic lengths.
- Respect the intended hierarchy.
- Simulate real corporate/investment content.
- Help test typography, spacing, cards, sections, and responsive behavior.

Placeholder content must be easy to replace later without redesigning the interface.

Final content and real partner logos will be provided and reviewed later.

---

## 8. Visual Positioning

The website should feel:

- Premium
- Institutional
- Trustworthy
- Elegant
- Powerful
- Stable
- Modern
- Investment-oriented
- Saudi / regional in character

Avoid:

- Generic corporate templates
- Startup-style visual language
- Excessive animations
- Overly colorful interfaces
- Service-agency appearance
- Unnecessary visual decoration
- Overly complicated layouts

The visual language should communicate ZAN as a serious company dealing with investments, projects, partnerships, and major organizations.

---

## 9. Existing Brand References

The current ZAN company profile and logo are the primary visual references.

The website should extract the visual DNA of the existing materials rather than copy the company profile literally.

Main visual characteristics:

- Dark navy
- Gold
- White
- Geometric logo
- Premium/institutional atmosphere
- Investment and architectural imagery
- Strong visual hierarchy
