# ZAN Website — Design Direction

## Overall Direction

ZAN should have a premium institutional visual identity suitable for a Saudi investment company.

The website should communicate:

- Trust
- Authority
- Stability
- Investment capability
- Professionalism
- Growth
- Elegance
- Strategic thinking

The design should feel expensive and established without becoming excessive or decorative.

---

## Visual Character

Keywords:

Premium
Institutional
Elegant
Confident
Modern
Minimal
Powerful
Saudi
Investment-focused

---

## Color Direction

Primary visual palette:

- Deep Navy
- Gold
- White
- Soft neutral backgrounds

Navy should provide the main institutional foundation.

Gold should be used as an accent to communicate premium positioning.

Gold must NOT dominate the interface.

White and neutral surfaces should provide visual breathing room.

Exact values are defined in design-system.md and may be adjusted after sampling the official logo.


---

## Imagery Direction

Use high-quality imagery related to:

- Saudi Arabia
- Architecture
- Business
- Investment
- Hospitality
- Real estate
- Infrastructure
- Modern cities
- Strategic partnerships
- Economic development

Avoid generic corporate stock imagery whenever possible.

Images should feel cinematic, sophisticated, and relevant to the investment context.


---

## Layout Direction

Use:

- Large visual sections
- Strong typography
- Generous whitespace
- Clear hierarchy
- Controlled grid systems
- Large imagery
- Structured cards
- Strong but restrained CTAs

Avoid:

- Crowded layouts
- Too many cards in one section
- Excessive borders
- Excessive rounded corners
- Excessive shadows
- Decorative elements without purpose


---

## Navigation

The navigation should feel:

- Clean
- Premium
- Stable
- Easy to understand

The logo should remain prominent.

The primary CTA should be clearly visible.

The language switcher should support:

- Arabic
- English
- Russian


---

## Typography

Typography should be modern, professional, highly readable, and compatible with:

- Arabic
- English
- Russian

Arabic typography should feel premium but remain highly readable.

English and Russian typography should maintain the same visual personality.


---

## Components

Buttons:
- Strong and clean
- Minimal decoration
- Clear hierarchy

Cards:
- Structured
- Elegant
- Moderate radius
- Subtle borders or shadows
- Strong image/text hierarchy

Sections:
- Strong headings
- Clear spacing
- Consistent vertical rhythm


---

## Animation

Animation should be subtle and purposeful.

Use:

- Fade
- Slide
- Gentle reveal
- Small hover transitions

Avoid:

- Excessive motion
- Long animations
- Distracting effects
- Animation on every element

The website should feel calm and confident.


---

## Design Principle

The design should communicate:

"ZAN is a serious investment company with the capability, relationships, and vision to develop and manage opportunities."

It should NOT communicate:

"We are a flashy digital agency."
